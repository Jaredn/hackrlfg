# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Compact group browser with a fixed Tank / Healer / DPS / DPS / DPS layout, class icons and levels.
- Split-colour chips for solo players who signed up for several roles; they always take a Tank or Healer slot when they offer one.
- Two rows of slots for 10-player groups (UBRS) and raids, with a role headcount.
- Searches every dungeon in a category, not just the ones in your level range.
- Dungeon picker: all, around my level, or one dungeon.
- Filter by player, dungeon or zone; sort by age.
- List yourself, update or remove your listing from a small panel.
- Click to whisper, shift-click to invite solo players.
